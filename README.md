# team_strawberry_game2
Our second collaborative GameMaker Studio game project created by Team Strawberry: Lilian, Lasya, Hannah, and Randall

==================================================
YUM YUM BLAZE!
==================================================

The camera pans into a TV cooking show studio. You are a contestant
who must prove to the 3 judges that you are the best chef in the
competition. Win all three rounds and you take home a life-changing
amount of money!

--------------------------------------------------
HOW TO PLAY
--------------------------------------------------
Goal: Finish all 3 rounds with enough points to beat your opponents
and win the show.

Rounds (one minigame each):
  Round 1 - DRINK   : Create a drink by using lemons, sugar, and water.
  Round 2 - ENTREE  : Create an entree by prepping a variety of ingredients.
  Round 3 - DESSERT : Create a dessert by baking and whipping ingredients.

Each round:
1. You are given ingredients and a recipe for a dish (or drink).
   Each dish uses about 2 ingredients.
2. A ticket in the TOP RIGHT of the screen shows the order the
   ingredients should be placed in. Follow it!
   - If you place them in the wrong order, the dish is still made,
     but you lose points.
3. Complete the minigame as quickly and accurately as you can before
   the time runs out.
4. The host will make occasional comments such as time warnings
   ("30 seconds remaining!"), performance feedback, and fun facts.
5. After the minigame, your dish is scored.

Example dishes:
  Drink   - Lemonade (Water, Lemon)
  Entree  - Sushi (Seaweed & Rice, Salmon)
  Dessert - Strawberry Milkshake (Strawberry Ice Cream, Milk)

--------------------------------------------------
SCORING
--------------------------------------------------
Points are based on:
  - The base points of each ingredient
  - How fast the dish is completed
  - How accurate you were (correct order, well-timed cuts, etc.)

Winning a round:
  - If your score is BELOW the minimum needed, your opponents end up
    with more points than you and you must RETRY the round.
  - If your score is ABOVE the minimum, you beat your opponents,
    win the round, and move on to the next one.
  - Example: with a minimum of 500 points, scoring under 500 means
    the opponents score over 500; scoring over 500 means they score
    below you.
  - Pass all 3 rounds and you become the winner of the show!

SPECIAL SAUCE (Round 2 - Entree):
  During the entree round you may ACCEPT or REJECT a "special sauce."
  It is random: it can help your dish (judges raise your final score)
  or hurt it (judges lower your final score). Risk it or play it safe!

CONTROLS
Use a mouse to click and drag ingredients, to cut up or mix, etc.

CHEAT CODES
